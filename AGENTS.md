# CPR Sanjeevani Certificate Access System Memory
- **Drive-Based Certificates**: Certificates are pre-generated and stored on Google Drive. Individual drive links are provided in CSV files placed inside `cprcertificates/`.
- **Participant Access**: Participants search for their certificate by Certificate ID, Mobile Number, or Email ID.
- **View & Download Only**: Certificates are accessed, viewed, and downloaded directly via Google Drive links.
- **No HTML Generation**: Under no circumstances should HTML certificates be generated dynamically for this section.
